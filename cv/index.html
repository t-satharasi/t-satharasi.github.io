Professional Summary

Aerospace engineering researcher interested in mathematically rigorous control, nonlinear and adaptive control, safety-critical control, system identification, and learning-based methods for aerospace and robotic systems. My work focuses on developing analytical methods with provable stability, safety, and convergence guarantees, together with simulation and experimental validation.
