I received my M.S. in Aerospace Engineering from the University of Florida in 2026 and my B.Tech. in Aerospace Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Madras in 2024. During my undergraduate studies, I worked as an Undergraduate Research Assistant at the National Centre for Combustion Research and Development, contributing to experimental design, setup, and analysis. During my master’s, I conducted research in the Systems, Cognition, and Control Laboratory, focusing primarily on the mathematical foundations of control systems. My research interests include nonlinear and adaptive control, safety-critical control, system identification, and game-theoretic methods for control and decision-making. I am particularly interested in developing rigorous analytical methods with provable stability and safety guarantees, while also studying how these methods translate to aerospace and robotic systems through simulation and experiments.
